# Trip priorities — what we value & how we rate it

Single source of truth for **what to hunt, what to skip, and how important a place is** on the Korea/Taiwan trip. Consulted by two skills and usable on its own to score any candidate:

- **`find-places`** ranks a research shortlist against **§A The priority ladder**.
- **`add-place`** assigns the **§B importance star rating** and reuses the same ladder to place a category.

Don't restate the ladder or the rubric inside the skills — link here and keep this file the only copy. Emoji **type-tags are defined once** in the **Emoji Key at the top of `places.md`**; that stays canonical and §C only points to it.

## Audience baseline

**Culture-forward, very active, with kids ages 9 / 11 / 15** (3 adults + 3 kids, two groups — see the `family-composition` memory). **Overpack** — this family would rather drop a stop on the spot than under-plan; when in doubt surface more and mark the lowest-priority ones as the natural drops, never thin to a "normal" pace. **Nightlife / bars are LOW interest even for the adults** — only if explicitly asked, and then minimal. Kids never lower the culture bar toward gimmicks. Price by **age tier** (9 & 11 usually child; 15 often youth/student/adult; many venues free under a cutoff) — never a flat "child".

## A. The priority ladder — what to hunt vs. exclude

An ordered ladder of what we're hunting for. Rank every candidate against it rather than taking whatever a source lists first — culture is the main goal, NOT Instagram. When torn between a gimmick and a genuine cultural venue, pick the genuine one.

1. **HIGHEST — actively seek out, rank above general sightseeing:** modern/contemporary-art museums and **fine-art photography** museums/galleries. Anchors: **Museum Hanmi** & **Photo SeMA** (Seoul), **GoEun Museum of Photography** (Busan), **National Center of Photography and Images** (Taipei); plus TFAM / MoCA and leading contemporary galleries (Kukje, etc.). **"Photo gallery" = a gallery of _photographs_ (photography as an art form), never a photo-op backdrop.**
2. **HIGH — museums & galleries in general.** 4–7 in one day is normal and welcome; never skip one for "too many museums". Include folk/history/science/design and university/private galleries.
3. **HIGH — science, technology, industry & transport (STEM + "how things are made / how things work").** This family skews hands-on and loves machines and trains — hunt these as first-class targets, not filler; rank alongside general museums (tier 2), above core sightseeing when a venue is genuinely engaging. These are real education, **not** photo-ops — never tag them 🤳. Suggested tags: 🔬 science/tech · 🏭 factory/industry · 🚢 ships/maritime · 🚗 cars · 🚂 railway · ✈️ aviation · 🔭 observatory/planetarium (add these to the `places.md` Emoji Key when first used).
   - **Science & technology museums / science centers** — interactive STEM, IMAX/planetarium domes, live demos. Anchors: National Science Museum (Gwacheon) & the Seoul/Gwacheon science centers; **National Taiwan Science Education Center** (Taipei), **National Museum of Natural Science** (Taichung), **National Science & Technology Museum** (Kaohsiung), Busan Science Museum.
   - **Transport — cars, rail, aviation, maritime:**
     - **Automobile / brand museums & maker experiences** — the Toyota Commemorative Museum (Nagoya) is the archetype; hunt the *local* equivalent — e.g. **Hyundai Motorstudio** (Seoul/Goyang), Samsung Transportation Museum (Yongin). Tag 🚗.
     - **Railway heritage & heritage/scenic train rides** — railway museums, roundhouses, depots, rideable heritage lines. Already in play: National Railway Museum (Taipei), Takao Railway Museum (Kaohsiung), Fan-Shaped Train Garage (Taichung), the Pingxi/Shifen line. Tag 🚂.
     - **Aviation & aerospace** — aviation/aerospace museums, airport observation decks. Tag ✈️.
     - **Ships & maritime** — boardable historic warships/submarines, naval memorials, maritime museums, tall ships, working harbours & fish-auction floors, lighthouses. Anchor: **Korea National Maritime Museum** (Busan). Tag 🚢.
   - **Working factories & "tourism factories" with public tours** — production you can walk through, ideally with a make-your-own session. **Taiwan's 觀光工廠 (guānguāng gōngchǎng, "tourism factory") network is a whole category:** pineapple-cake, instant-noodle, fish-ball, ceramics (Yingge), glass (Tittot), soy sauce, plus distilleries/breweries (Kavalan whisky). Korea: kimchi/tteok/soju/beer & K-beauty maker tours, Paju printing/publishing. Screen for advance booking + a hands-on option. Tag 🏭 (add 🎨 if there's a DIY workshop).
   - **Industrial & mining heritage** — mines, dockyards, refineries, kilns, and power/infrastructure sites turned museum or park. Anchors: Gold Museum (Jinguashi) + Pingxi/Jingtong coal-mining relics, Pier-2/Takao (Kaohsiung), reclaimed plants like Seoul's Seonyudo (old water-treatment) & Oil Tank Culture Park. Tag 🏭 (add 🚶 for a walkable site).
   - **Also fits this family** (search + rank normally, real museums not 🤳): **planetariums/observatories** (🔭), **money/printing museums** (Bank of Korea Money Museum), **robot & retro-arcade/game museums**, **toy/brick/model & miniatures museums** (Miniatures Museum, Taipei), and **big engineering you can visit** — dams, notable bridges, locks, lighthouses.
4. **CORE sightseeing:**
   - Palaces/temples/historic · parks/gardens/nature · viewpoints/decks/towers · genuine landmarks · major theme parks.
   - **Character-rich districts & neighborhoods** — arts/design/hip quarters and atmospheric old towns worth wandering *as a whole* (SoHo-style: e.g. Seoul's Ikseon-dong / Seongsu, Busan's Gamcheon). Hunt for the *district itself*, not only a single stop in it. Tag 🚶.
   - **Street art & murals** — mural villages, street-art alleys, public-art installations (e.g. Gamcheon Culture Village, Ihwa Mural Village). Usually free; good culture between stops. Tag 🎨 (add 🚶 if it's a walkable route).
   - Night markets · genuine/atmospheric markets · strolls & old streets.
   - **A plain shopping mall or outlet is NOT a stop on its own** — we're not interested in malls for shopping. Include one only when it's a real cultural/architectural draw or landmark in its own right (e.g. a giant Gundam-style icon out front, Zaha Hadid's DDP, the Starfield COEX library) — never for the retail. This applies to outlets too (e.g. skip Doota, Hyundai City Outlet).
5. **Food & drink — we like to EAT ON THE GO.** Prioritize handheld / grab-and-go: street food, **market & night-market stalls**, bakeries and snack counters, anything you can eat while walking. Signature **no-chain** sit-down spots (local-legend, Michelin/Bib-Gourmand, historic) are welcome but secondary — favor the quick ones or a genuine destination. Distinctive cafés/dessert/tea houses/bubble tea for a pause. Skip chains and filler.
6. **Experiences & extras:** hands-on/cultural workshops · animals · hot springs/spas · scenic rides · performing arts. (Heritage/scenic train rides & factory make-your-own sessions cluster with tier 3.)
7. **LOW priority — search, but rank low and flag as instagrammable (tag 🤳):** immersive / projection "art" spaces — teamLab (https://www.teamlab.art/), Arte Museum by d'strict (https://artemuseum.com/), Bunker / Théâtre des Lumières (https://www.bunkerdelumieres.com/), and similar. We're **mostly not interested** in these — include them so the option exists, never above a real museum/gallery, and mark them 🤳 so they read as photo-experiences, not serious culture.

**Hard exclusions — never surface as culture or as a museum/gallery substitute:** trick-eye / trick-art / 3D-illusion museums, selfie museums, optical-illusion & AR photo-op spots (e.g. Trickeye, Alive Museum). At most keep **one** as a minor kids' optional, clearly labeled as a selfie spot. Also skip: chains, generic dish bullets, nightlife, and anything permanently closed or superseded.

## B. The importance star rating (1–5 ★, "regret-if-missed")

Every real destination carries a 1–5 importance rating — how much you'd regret missing it, for *this* culture-forward, active, kids-9+ trip.

| ★ | Meaning |
|---|---------|
| ★★★★★ | Unmissable, world-class/defining sight or flagship full-day attraction (major theme parks = 5). |
| ★★★★☆ | High-priority highlight, top of its category. |
| ★★★☆☆ | Worth-it-if-nearby; kid-magnet themed spots; signature single restaurants. |
| ★★☆☆☆ | Nice-to-have, interchangeable café/shop/minor park. |
| ★☆☆☆☆ | Marginal / redundant for a culture-forward, active, kids-9+ trip. |

- **Bump kid-magic spots up one notch.**
- **Rank the category against §A first**, then rate: tier-1/2/3 venues (photography & contemporary art, general museums, STEM/industry/transport) rate generously — rarely below ★★★☆☆; tier-7 immersive-projection spaces rank low; hard-exclusion trick-eye/selfie "museums" cap ~★★☆☆☆ and are labelled selfie spots, never treated as galleries.
- **Rate every real destination** (linked or a named venue). Do **not** rate generic dish bullets or the "Must-Try Dishes" checklists.

**Notation:** always 5 glyphs — **★★★★★ / ★★★★☆ / ★★★☆☆ / ★★☆☆☆ / ★☆☆☆☆** — placed as a prefix **inside the link text, before the emoji type-tags**, e.g. `- [★★★★★ 🏯🎨 Gyeongbokgung Palace](url)`. Unlinked named-venue bullets get the stars at the start of the bullet text. The stars are cosmetic to the maps saver (its query comes from the URL) and render in the display name on the site.
