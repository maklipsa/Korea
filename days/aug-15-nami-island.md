# Aug 15 (Sat) — LIBERATION DAY: NAMI ISLAND + GAPYEONG
> Badges: holiday:Liberation Day

National holiday. Get out of the city — nature day with kid-friendly activities.

**Transport (big travel day — no car needed):** All trains + tour bus — ITX-Cheongchun (Yongsan ⇄ Gapyeong, reserve both ways), the **Gapyeong City Tour Bus** loop for Nami, and one Korail stop east to Gimyujeong (Kim Yu-jeong Village) plus a taxi hop to the **Soyang Dam pier** for the Cheongpyeongsa ferry. A couple of taxis (2 cars for 6) cover the Gimyujeong ⇄ Soyang Dam legs.

**Tight day — and it's Liberation Day.** Big stops spread across a wide area (Nami/Gapyeong → Gimyujeong / Soyang Dam **northeast** → Garden of Morning Calm back **west**) = run-from-thing-to-thing pace with backtracking. Aug 15 is a national holiday on a **Saturday**, so Gapyeong is one of the busiest day trips in the country: **book the ITX-Cheongchun both ways well ahead**, expect long ferry/zipwire queues at Nami, and buy Nami tickets online. Realistic call: **Nami + Cheongpyeongsa fills the day** — Garden of Morning Calm / Petite France is the natural drop (off-season anyway, and a big westward backtrack). Or flip it: skip Cheongpyeongsa and stay west for the garden if the Nami zipline was thrill enough and the northeast detour feels like too much.

**Transport tip:** the **Gapyeong City Tour Bus** (hop-on-hop-off, ~8,000 KRW adult / ~6,000 child) loops Gapyeong Station ↔ Nami ↔ Garden of Morning Calm — handy if you take the western garden option, but it does **not** serve Gimyujeong, the Soyang Dam pier, or the Chuncheon stops (reach Gimyujeong by Korail from Gapyeong, then taxi to the pier; the Samaksan cable car / Chuncheon National Museum are in Chuncheon, one–two ITX stops on, with the ITX-Cheongchun home from Chuncheon/Namchuncheon instead of Gapyeong).

## Also Nearby

- [★★★★☆ 🚡🧒 Gangchon Rail Bike](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Gangchon+Rail+Bike+Gapyeong) — 8km pedal-powered rail cars through river valleys and tunnels from **Gimyujeong Station**; sessions 10:00 / 11:30 / 13:30 / 15:00 / 16:30 / 18:00, ~35,000 KRW / 4-seater, reserve ahead on the holiday weekend. **Swap it in for Cheongpyeongsa** if the kids would rather have a ride than a temple — it starts at the same Gimyujeong stop, so it's a clean substitution.
- [★★★☆☆ 🗼📸 Soyanggang Skywalk (Chuncheon)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Soyanggang+Skywalk+Chuncheon) — Glass-floor pedestrian bridge over the Soyang River, near the eastern Chuncheon stops; free / nominal shoe-cover fee. A quick 20–30 min "scary-floor" thrill if you're already out east for the cable car or museum.
